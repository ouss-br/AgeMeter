Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how AgeMeter handles information when you visit and use our website.

1. Introduction

Welcome to AgeMeter. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use our website.

This Privacy Policy describes our information practices and applies to visitors who access or use AgeMeter.

2. Information We Collect

AgeMeter does not currently require users to create an account or register to use its age calculation tools.

The date of birth entered into our calculator is used by your browser to perform the requested calculations. The current calculator code does not send or save this date on an AgeMeter server.

When you contact us through the contact form, your email application may use the information you provide to prepare a message.

When the website is hosted, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and server request data, according to its own policies.

3. Age Calculation and Birth Dates

AgeMeter uses JavaScript running in your browser to calculate your age, total time lived, and birthday countdown.

Based on the current calculator implementation, the date you enter is processed locally in your browser and is not transmitted to our server.

You can use the calculator without creating an account.

4. How We Use Information

Information entered into AgeMeter's tools is used to provide the functionality requested by the visitor.

Information included in a contact message may be used to understand and respond to your inquiry, if you choose to send it.

5. Contact Form

Our current contact form uses your device's email application through a mailto link.

Submitting the form opens an email draft. The message is not automatically sent by AgeMeter, and our website does not currently store contact messages in a database.

If you choose to send the email, its processing will also be subject to the policies of your email provider.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current JavaScript code used for AgeMeter's calculator and navigation does not set cookies.

Cookies and similar technologies may be introduced by third-party services in the future, including advertising or analytics providers.

If such services are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant technologies and their purposes.

7. Advertising

AgeMeter does not currently display Google AdSense advertisements.

If Google AdSense is introduced, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

These technologies may be used to measure advertising performance and provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, depending on the applicable settings and user consent.

Users can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings .

More information about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services is available in Google's information about partner sites .

This section will be updated if advertising services are activated on AgeMeter.

8. Third-Party Services

AgeMeter may use third-party services to support website hosting, analytics, advertising, or other website functionality.

These third-party providers may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and practices.

When third-party services are introduced, we will update this policy with relevant information about those services.

9. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect information handled through AgeMeter.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

The current age calculator does not save the birth date entered by visitors.

Messages prepared through the contact form are handled by the visitor's email application. AgeMeter does not currently maintain a database of these messages.

11. Children's Privacy

AgeMeter is a general-purpose website and does not currently provide user accounts or registration.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration or user profiles.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion.

You may contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AgeMeter develops or introduces new features and services.

Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated revision date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how AgeMeter handles information, please visit our Contact Us page.
